# Custom enchantments

Every custom enchant on Crispy Anarchy and Crispy PvP (the `crispy_enchants` datapack, rebuilt from the old
AdvancedEnchantments setup), grouped by rarity tier. Descriptions come from the original AdvancedEnchantments config
and the Enchanter's book text. The datapack rebuild may differ in small details such as exact chances or numbers.

**Getting them**:
- On Anarchy, buy random books from the Enchanter in the market with XP.
- On PvP, admins can use the admin room (`/function pvp:admin/room`).
- Ops anywhere can run `/enchant @s crispy_enchants:<id> <level>`, or
  `/function crispy_enchants:admin/give {e:"<id>",l:<level>}` for a book.

Heroic enchants are upgrades: each needs its base enchant at max level (see its description).
