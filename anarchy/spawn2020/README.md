# Crispy Anarchy 2020 spawn (rebuild)

No world copy or schematic of the 2020 spawn survived, so `build.py` recreates it from the one screenshot we have
(`compare.png`: the screenshot next to a preview of the rebuild).

- `python3 build.py spawn2020.schem` writes the WorldEdit schematic (128 x 160, 72 tall). It lives on Anarchy as
  `config/worldedit/schematics/spawn2020.schem`: `//schem load spawn2020`, then `//paste` (the origin is the middle of
  the south wall's gate at ground level; `//paste -a` skips air).
- Walled square with CRISPY ANARCHY in sea lanterns on the wall tops (the original lettering can't be read), a gate in
  the south wall, grass at the near end and a snowy plaza beyond.
- Builds (left to right, near to far): flat-roofed wooden hall with a fox pixel-art billboard, grass with flowers and
  lights, white mansion with a deepslate roof; pond with a dock, glass dome garden, ribbed pumpkin, wooden hut, teal
  prismarine pavilion; fenced poppy field with dead trees; shops, market awnings and black roads; red nether-brick
  spire; white sandstone tower with a beacon and a floating red ring; grey tower with a saucer top; Japanese houses,
  pink wool trees, spruces and wheat farms.
- Everything not visible in the screenshot (backs, interiors, the far side) is invented.
