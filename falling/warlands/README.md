# The Warlands (concept mockup, not built)

A proposed high-risk / high-reward PvP resource island for Crispy Skyblock: `mockup.py <out>` builds a voxel model
of it and renders `warlands_iso.png` (isometric view) and `warlands_plan.png` (labelled plan). Nothing here is in the
game yet.

- Scorched floating island (~66 blocks radius) in the void: blackstone, basalt and ash, lava cracks and a lava river.
- Arrival bastion (south, safe): obsidian platform, hub portal, gate wall; a bridge over the chasm into the warzone.
- PvP on past the gate; combat tag blocks /warp, /island, /hub; dying drops everything carried.
- Four resource fields (richest ores in Skyblock, regrowing like the other zones): diamond/emerald quarry (NE),
  ancient debris pit by the lava river (W), gold/amethyst geodes (NW), crystal ridge (E). Mobs Lv 25-40.
- Treasure Node (centre): raised platform with an obelisk and beacon; charges every 20 min, announced server-wide;
  hold the platform 30 s to loot it.
- Cover: ruined walls, broken pillars, four rim watchtowers.
- War Spoils: Warlands-only currency for exclusive gear.
